## Caveats

- The dataset reflects **complaints filed**, not adjudicated wrongdoing. A
  company appearing high on a complaint leaderboard may simply be a large
  servicer with proportionally more customers.
- The 90-day window means seasonality (e.g., year-end mortgage cycles) is not
  visible. The longer-window report will surface those patterns.
- Consumer-narrative text is excluded from this build to keep the model lean;
  see the bulk-download path in `data/README.md` if you need it.
